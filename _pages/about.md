## What I’m working on
- **Multimodal Graph Meta-Transformer (MGMT):** integrating modality-specific graph transformers with a meta-graph that captures cross-modal relationships for robust, interpretable predictions.  
- **Selective Bayesianization for Transformers:** injecting Bayesian structure into targeted layers/heads to calibrate uncertainty without sacrificing speed.  
- **Agentic AI for Operations:** multi-agent planning (planner–executor–critic loops) for enterprise and scientific pipelines.
