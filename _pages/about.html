01

Bayesian ML & uncertainty

I develop efficient Bayesian inference and conformal methods for predictions whose uncertainty is useful and well calibrated.

2026 / Preprint

SCOPE Before You Sample: Conformal Scores Guide Posterior Exploration for Fast and Calibrated Bayesian Predictive Inference

Zahra Moslemi, Ziyi Liang, Babak Shahbaba

arXiv preprint · Under review at AISTATS 2027

Conformal scores identify the posterior directions that matter for predictive regions, combining targeted MCMC with a variational approximation and calibrated coverage.

Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR)

A calibration–emulation–sampling strategy accelerates Bayesian neural network inference while retaining predictive accuracy and uncertainty estimation.

02

Biomedical AI & AI for science

I study graph representations and interpretable learning across heterogeneous data, with applications in neural decoding and biomedical prediction.

2026 / Preprint

Heterogeneous Graph Alignment for Joint Reasoning and Interpretability

Zahra Moslemi, Ziyi Liang, Norbert Fortin, Babak Shahbaba

arXiv preprint · Multi-Graph Meta-Transformer (MGMT)

A shared meta-graph aligns attention-selected supernodes across heterogeneous graphs, supporting joint prediction and interpretable scientific analysis. Related MGMT work was presented at the NeurIPS 2025 NPGML Workshop (Spotlight).

NeurIPS 2025 · AI4Science Workshop · Spotlight · Best Poster Runner-up
